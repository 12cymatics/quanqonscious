QuanQonscious Simulation Suite

Version 1.0.2. Two simulators that run in your browser, offline, with nothing to install.

Vedic Sutra Simulator

The 29 methods of Tirthaji's Vedic Mathematics acting on a 16-point tesseract field, calculated in exact arithmetic and drawn as currents and flux on a torus.

Faraday Cell

A three-dimensional nonlinear Navier–Stokes solver for the free surface of a vibrated liquid in a circular cell, with the cell, the liquid and the drive set in SI units.
