1. Software

All program code in vedic-sutra-simulator.html and faraday-cell.html was
written for this project. Neither page loads a script, font, style sheet or
data file from anywhere else, and neither contains a third-party library.


2. Vedic Sutra Simulator

The 29 methods are those of:

  Jagadguru Swami Sri Bharati Krishna Tirthaji Maharaja,
  "Vedic Mathematics", ed. V. S. Agrawala, Motilal Banarsidass, 1965.

The simulator implements the mathematical methods and uses the sutras'
names. It reproduces no passage of the book. Worked examples used to check
the methods were taken from:

  Rajesh Kumar Thakur, "Advanced Vedic Mathematics", Rupa, 2019.

Those examples are used in the project's own checks, which are not part of
this package. Errors found in the printed examples were corrected before use.


3. Faraday Cell

The equations solved are the incompressible Navier-Stokes equations with a
free surface. They are standard physics and belong to no one.

The optional apparatus presets and the optional pattern atlas are taken from:

  Merlin Sheldrake and Rupert Sheldrake, "Determinants of Faraday Wave-Patterns
  in Water Samples Oscillated Vertically at a Range of Frequencies from 50-200
  Hz", WATER 9, 2017. DOI 10.14294/WATER.2017.6

What is embedded in faraday-cell.html from that paper:
  - the three cell diameters and the drive settings it reports;
  - for each frequency from 50 to 200 Hz, the fold symmetry the paper printed
    for each photograph, and statistics measured from those photographs
    (brightness, contrast, and radial and angular harmonics).
No photograph and no text of the paper is included.
