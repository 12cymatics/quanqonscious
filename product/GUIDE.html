Running it

Extract the ZIP, open the folder and open START-HERE.html in a desktop browser. Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari all work. Nothing is installed, nothing is downloaded and no account is needed. Both simulators run entirely on your computer and send nothing anywhere.

The pictures need WebGL2, which every current desktop browser has. A file preview that does not run JavaScript, such as the one in a phone's Files app or an email attachment viewer, shows the controls but nothing moves. Open the file in the browser itself.

Vedic Sutra Simulator

The simulator holds a field of 16 numbers, one on each corner of a tesseract. Every number is an exact fraction, never a rounded decimal. The 29 methods of Bharati Krishna Tirthaji's Vedic Mathematics, 16 sutras and 13 sub-sutras, each read their operands from the field, work their method exactly as the book does, and turn the field by the result. Around them run the magnetism, vortex and drive stages. The torus shows the field: its shape follows the field, the lines on it are the currents along the 32 edges, and the patches are the flux through the 16 cells.

What you see at the start

Every sutra is off. The vortex is at 3 and the drive at 1, so currents flow from the first step. The model takes one step each second.

The controls

LATTICE, FIELDS, CHARTSShow or hide the currents and flux on the surface, the field lines around it, and the charts.

Switching a sutra off

When a sutra is switched off, what it put into the field is taken back out over the glide, exactly. With the vortex and the drive at 0, the field returns to the energy it started with.

The FAITHFUL badge

The badge reads FAITHFUL only while every part of the picture is drawn from the exact state and every check of that state holds. If any check fails the badge says so and the part it covers is withheld instead of being drawn wrongly.

What it is, and what it is not

  • It is an exact mathematical model. The arithmetic is exact fractions up to 4096 bits, then intervals that are guaranteed to contain the true value.
  • The 29 methods follow the book and are checked against worked examples from it.
  • The words magnetism, vortex, flux and current name parts of this model. It is not a simulation of a physical magnet or of any laboratory apparatus, and it has not been compared with an experiment.
  • It makes no claim about physics, medicine or consciousness.

Faraday Cell

A liquid in a circular cell is shaken vertically and its surface forms standing waves. This simulator solves the three-dimensional nonlinear Navier–Stokes equations for an incompressible Newtonian liquid with a free surface, a pinned rim and a no-slip floor and wall. Diameter, depth, density, viscosity, surface tension, gravity and the drive are entered in SI units.

Using it

  • The page draws the initial surface at time 0 and then starts the solver. Pause keeps the computed state; Reset returns to the initial condition and waits for Run.
  • Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom. Top view and 3D view move the camera.
  • The vertical scale starts at 5000× because the default displacement is microscopic next to the cell's diameter. Choose 1× for true scale. The scale changes only the drawing.
  • The sand-coloured particles are passive tracers carried by the computed velocity. They are not a model of sediment.
  • The physical clock and the measured wall time are both shown. The solver is not real-time at its accuracy grid: a millisecond of liquid time can take many seconds to compute.

What it is, and what it is not

  • It solves the full nonlinear equations on a finite grid. The page sizes the grid so the seeded mode is resolved to within 1% and refuses, with the numbers, when no grid of at most 250,000 cells can do that.
  • The surface is single-valued. There are no jets, droplets, air entrainment or overturning waves; the solver stops and says so when the surface would break.
  • It has not been compared with an experiment or with another published code. Its internal checks show the arithmetic is consistent; they do not validate it against the physical world.
  • The apparatus presets and the pattern atlas are optional comparisons taken from a published paper (see Sources and notices). They do not drive the solver.

Requirements

  • A desktop or laptop computer with a current browser.
  • WebGL2 for the pictures. All calculation is done on the processor in double precision or exact arithmetic.
  • No network connection.
