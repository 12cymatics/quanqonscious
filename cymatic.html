navier-stokes · Floquet stability in this cell

This optional modal diagnostic compares onset from a damped Mathieu form — one amplitude equation, with all the viscous damping folded into a single rate γ. This solves the incompressible Navier–Stokes equations instead, linearised about the flat layer in this disc: the same radius, depth, water and drive, with no slip on the floor and the sidewall, the full normal-stress balance at the free surface, and the contact line as the control above sets it. It integrates one drive period and takes the eigenvalues of that period map; |μ| > 1 is growth. Nothing is assumed about the damping — the viscosity enters as the diffusion term and the boundary layers are resolved by a graded grid.

General nonlinear experiments use the entered SI properties and acceleration. The paper atlas is optional comparison data and never selects nonlinear patterns. The 3D surface, flow tracers and recorded-frame exports share the computed physical state. Camera and explicit displacement magnification affect drawing only. Tracers do not model finite sand grains or sediment contacts. Free-surface overturning, gas dynamics and moving contact lines are outside this solver's scope.
