The deck above decides onset from a damped Mathieu form — one amplitude equation, with all the viscous damping folded into a single rate γ. This solves the incompressible Navier–Stokes equations instead, linearised about the flat layer in this disc: the same radius, depth, water and drive, with no slip on the floor and the sidewall, the full normal-stress balance at the free surface, and the contact line as the control above sets it. It integrates one drive period and takes the eigenvalues of that period map; |μ| > 1 is growth. Nothing is assumed about the damping — the viscosity enters as the diffusion term and the boundary layers are resolved by a graded grid.

Layers are kept separate. Fold labels are the paper's printed classifications. Contrast / centre-bias / ring / texture figures are image descriptors measured from the rendered PDF panels — renderer hints, never the authors' measurements. Bessel modes and water properties are theory, used for diagnostics and outside the atlas.
Amplitude stays in apparatus mV: the paper gives no mV→acceleration transfer function, so none is invented. Surface tension is IAPWS R1-76; viscosity is the Vogel–Andrade fit, not IAPWS.
