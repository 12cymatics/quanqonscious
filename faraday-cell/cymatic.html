This optional modal diagnostic compares onset from a damped Mathieu form — one amplitude equation, with
  all the viscous damping folded into a single rate γ. This solves the incompressible
  Navier–Stokes equations instead, linearised about the flat layer in this disc: the
  same radius, depth, water and drive, with no slip on the floor and the sidewall, the full
  normal-stress balance at the free surface, and the contact line as the control above sets
  it. It integrates one drive period and takes the eigenvalues of that period map; |μ| > 1
  is growth. Nothing is assumed about the damping — the viscosity enters as the diffusion
  term and the boundary layers are resolved by a graded grid.
