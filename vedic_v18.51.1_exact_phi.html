FIELDS AROUND THE CONSTRUCT

The construct's magnetism and its dielectric act on the space around it. Each edge carries the lattice current J = (d¹)ᵀB, the flux of the three faces round it (Ampère on the hypercube), and the current is conserved at every vertex. Each vertex carries the charge of the dielectric field, its graph Laplacian (Gauss), and the charges sum to zero. Placed where the surface puts them, they give the magnetic field by Biot–Savart and the electric field by Coulomb. The magnetism is seen as the pressure it exerts across its own field, p = |B|²/2: a medium whose index rises with that pressure fills the space round the construct, and everything is seen through it, bent toward the higher pressure the way heat over a hot car's paint moves what is behind it, with the medium itself a faint glow where the pressure along the line of sight is high. Across the equatorial plane, the glow is the energy density, its hue blue where the magnetic energy stands higher in its own range and orange where the electric does. The forces are I u × B on the middle of each piece of current, from the rest of the construct, and the electric force on each charge from the others, which cancel in sum. Each field is placed against its own range; nothing in the model ties their units together.
