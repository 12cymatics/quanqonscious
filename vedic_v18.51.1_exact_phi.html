FIELDS AROUND THE CONSTRUCT

The construct's magnetism and its dielectric act on the space around it. Each edge carries the lattice current J = (d¹)ᵀB, the flux of the three faces round it (Ampère on the hypercube), and the current is conserved at every vertex. Each vertex carries the charge of the dielectric field, its graph Laplacian (Gauss), and the charges sum to zero. Placed where the surface puts them, they give the magnetic field by Biot–Savart (blue lines) and the electric field by Coulomb (orange lines). Across the equatorial plane, the glow is the energy density, its hue blue where the magnetic energy stands higher in its own range and orange where the electric does, and the arrows are the energy flow S = E×B: where the dielectric's field and the magnetism exchange energy. The aqua arrows are the magnetic force I u × B on the middle of each piece of current, from the rest of the construct, always across the current and the field; the rose arrows the electric force on each charge from the others, which cancel in sum. Each field and force is drawn against its own range; the directions do not depend on the units.
