About the Amplituhedron

What is the Amplituhedron?

The amplituhedron is a geometric object discovered by Nima Arkani-Hamed and Jaroslav Trnka in 2013. It provides a revolutionary way to compute scattering amplitudes in quantum field theory without reference to spacetime or unitarity.

Scattering Amplitude = Volume of Amplituhedron

Key insights:

  • Locality and unitarity emerge from geometry
  • No Feynman diagrams needed
  • Positive Grassmannian structure
  • Dual conformal symmetry manifest

Momentum Twistors

Momentum twistors Zᵢ ∈ ℂℙ³ encode the kinematics of massless particles:

Zᵢ = (λᵢᵅ, μᵢᵅ̇) where μᵢ = xᵢᵅᵅ̇ λᵢₐ

The four-bracket ⟨ijkl⟩ = det(Zᵢ, Zⱼ, Zₖ, Zₗ) encodes all kinematic invariants.
